ABOUT ME

I'm Vaishak S, a film director and editor based in Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala.

I work across direction and post-production, with a particular interest in storytelling, editing, visual rhythm, colour and cinematic atmosphere.

From shaping a story on set to finding its final form in the edit, I enjoy being involved in the filmmaking process from idea to final frame.

A story doesn't end when the camera stops.

It's found again in the edit, in the rhythm, in the silence, and in every final frame.

Have a story to tell?

Let's make something worth watching.

Available for film direction, editing, music videos and creative collaborations.
